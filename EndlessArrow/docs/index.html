1 Introduction

Overview of the game and its core mechanics.

Arrow Challenge is a mobile puzzle game built with Flutter and Flame. Players tap winding arrow paths on a white game board and slide them off in the direction they point. The goal is to clear every arrow before the countdown timer reaches zero.

Each level is a dense maze of interlocking arrow snakes. Most arrows look movable but are blocked — you must discover the correct order to free them all. Wrong moves shake the board and cost hearts.

Key Features

  • 420+ Levels Procedurally generated, always solvable puzzles.
  • Timed Gameplay Countdown budget based on the arrow count — more arrows means more time, tightening per-arrow as levels advance.
  • 3 Hearts Lose a heart on every blocked move.
  • Sound Effects Swipe sound on successful arrow exits.
  • Progress Saving Unlocked levels and star ratings via SharedPreferences.
  • Pastel UI Cartoon logo, wooden controls, chunky buttons.
  • Google AdMob Banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads integrated.

3 Usages

How to play, customize, and use developer tools.

How to Play

  1. Splash Screen — Wait for the loading bar and read the tip.
  2. Menu — Tap PLAY to continue or LEVELS to pick a level.
  3. Goal — Remove every arrow from the board before the timer hits 0:00.
  4. Tap an arrow — If its exit path is clear, it slides off with a swipe sound.
  5. Blocked move — The board shakes, you lose a heart, and the arrow stays.
  6. Retry — Watch a rewarded ad via WATCH & RETRY after Time's Up or Out of Hearts.
  7. Win — Clear the board in time to earn stars and unlock the next level.

8 FAQ

Frequently asked questions about setup and gameplay.

Why don't ads show in the emulator?▼
AdMob ads require a physical Android or iOS device. They are disabled on web and desktop. Use flutter run --release on a real phone with test ad IDs during development.
How does the countdown timer work?▼
Level 1 starts with 20 seconds. Each level shaves time off linearly until the floor of 15 seconds at level 420. The timer turns red when 5 seconds or less remain.
Can I retry without watching an ad?▼
After Time's Up or Out of Hearts, retry requires watching a rewarded ad via the WATCH & RETRY button. Closing the ad early does not grant a retry. You can always tap reload to restart the level from scratch (costs progress on that attempt).
Are all 420 levels guaranteed solvable?▼
Yes. The generator in puzzle_generator.dart only places an arrow when its exit path is clear, then puzzle_validator.dart confirms a full solve. Run dart run tool/check_levels.dart to verify all 420 levels independently.
How do I change the app icon?▼
Replace assets/images/app_logo.png, then run dart run icons_launcher:create. The icons_launcher config is in pubspec.yaml.
Where do I put my real AdMob IDs?▼
Update lib/services/ad_config.dart for ad unit IDs, AndroidManifest.xml for the Android App ID, and ios/Runner/Info.plist for the iOS App ID (GADApplicationIdentifier).
Why does the board shake on some taps?▼
A shake means the arrow's exit path is blocked by another snake or the board edge. You lose one heart. Find an arrow whose entire path to the edge is clear.
